Quran Animals · Book 3

Namla the Ant

Together We Are Strong

  • Ages 5–8
  • English / French / Russian
  • Surah An-Naml · Chapter 27

She was tiny. But her voice changed everything.

Join Yusuf and Maryam as they meet Namla — a brave little ant who warned her entire community when the great army of Prophet Sulaiman approached. Her story is written in the Holy Quran in Surah An-Naml (Chapter 27 — "The Ants").

In this book children will discover

  • The amazing story of the ant and Prophet Sulaiman
  • That even the smallest voice matters to Allah
  • The importance of caring for others in our community
  • The exact words of Allah in Quran 27:18–19

Includes: Quran verses, fun facts about ants, discussion questions, colouring pages and activities.
