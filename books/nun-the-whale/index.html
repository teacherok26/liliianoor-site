Nun the Whale

Never Lose Hope

  • Ages 5–8
  • English / French / Russian
  • Surah Al-Anbiya · Chapter 21

The darkest place. The most powerful prayer.

In this book Yusuf and Maryam fall asleep by the sea — and in their dream they witness the incredible story of Prophet Yunus inside the belly of the whale. Three days. Three nights. Complete darkness. And one prayer that reached Allah.

In this book children will discover

  • The story of Prophet Yunus and the whale
  • That Allah always hears our prayers — even from the deepest darkness
  • The prayer of Prophet Yunus (Quran 21:87)
  • Why we should never lose hope in Allah

Includes: Quran verse, fun facts about whales, discussion questions, colouring pages and activities.
