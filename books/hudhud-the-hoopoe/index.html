Quran Animals · Book 5

Hudhud the Hoopoe

Always Be Honest

  • Ages 5–8
  • English / French / Russian
  • Surah An-Naml · Chapter 27

He was small. He was afraid. But he told the truth anyway.

In this book Yusuf and Maryam fall asleep under the stars — and in their dream they grow wings and fly alongside Hudhud the hoopoe bird on his most important mission: delivering a letter from Prophet Sulaiman to a faraway queen.

In this book children will discover

  • The story of Hudhud in Surah An-Naml (Chapter 27)
  • That honesty is always the right path — even when it feels scary
  • How the smallest messenger can carry the most important news
  • The letter of Prophet Sulaiman (Quran 27:30)

Includes: Quran verses, fun facts about hoopoe birds, discussion questions, colouring pages and activities.
