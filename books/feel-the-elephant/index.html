Quran Animals · Book 4

Feel the Elephant

Pride Goes Away

  • Ages 5–8
  • English / French / Russian
  • Surah Al-Fil · Chapter 105

A mighty army. Tiny birds. And one brave elephant who chose to do what was right.

In this book Yusuf and Maryam fall asleep in the garden — and in their dream they are transported to an ancient valley where they witness the powerful story of Surah Al-Fil (Chapter 105 — "The Elephant").

In this book children will discover

  • The story of the army of elephants and the birds Ababeel
  • How Allah protects what is sacred
  • The courage of Mahmud — the elephant who refused to dishonour Allah's house
  • Why true strength belongs only to Allah
  • The complete Surah Al-Fil (5 verses)

Includes: Surah Al-Fil, fun facts about elephants, discussion questions, colouring pages, cut & paste activities.
