Ankabut the Spider

Allah Protects You

  • Ages 5–8
  • English / French / Russian
  • Surah Al-Ankabut · Chapter 29

His web looks strong. But is it really?

Join Yusuf and Maryam as they meet Ankabut — a big fluffy black spider whose web holds one of the most powerful lessons in the Quran: that true protection comes only from Allah.

In this book children will discover

  • What Allah says about the spider's web in Surah Al-Ankabut (Chapter 29)
  • Why we should put our trust only in Allah — not in things that can break
  • The beloved Islamic tradition of the spider who protected the Prophet ﷺ in the cave (clearly marked as tradition, not Quran)
  • The exact words of Allah in Quran 29:41

Includes: Quran verse, fun facts about spiders, discussion questions, colouring pages and activities.
