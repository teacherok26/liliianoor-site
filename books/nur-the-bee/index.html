Nur the Bee

A Gift from Allah

  • Ages 5–8
  • English / French / Russian
  • Surah An-Nahl · Chapter 16

Did you know that the bee has its very own chapter in the Holy Quran?

Join Yusuf and Maryam as they meet Nur — a golden bee who shares the beautiful story of Surah An-Nahl (Chapter 16) and teaches children that hard work is a gift from Allah.

In this book children will discover

  • Why Allah gave the bee her own Surah
  • The miracle of honey mentioned in the Quran 1,400 years ago
  • How to find strength in Allah when things feel hard
  • The exact words of Allah in Quran 16:68–69

Includes: Quran verse, fun facts about bees, discussion questions, colouring pages and activities.
