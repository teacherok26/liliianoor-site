Quran Animals · Book 2

Ghuraab the Crow

Learn from Others

  • Ages 5–8
  • English / French / Russian
  • Surah Al-Maidah · Chapter 5

A wise crow. An important lesson. And a story written in the Holy Quran forever.

Join Yusuf and Maryam as they meet Ghuraab — a glossy black crow whose ancestor taught one of the most powerful lessons in the Quran: that wisdom can come from the most unexpected places.

In this book children will discover

  • The story of the crow in Surah Al-Maidah (Chapter 5)
  • Why staying humble helps us learn and grow
  • That Allah can send wisdom through any of His creatures
  • The exact words of Allah in Quran 5:31

Includes: Quran verse, fun facts about crows, discussion questions, colouring pages and activities.
