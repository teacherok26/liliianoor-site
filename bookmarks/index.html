الْإِخْلَاص
Al-Ikhlas
Sincerity · الْإِخْلَاص

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ﴿١﴾ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ﴿٢﴾ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ﴿٣﴾ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ ﴿٤﴾

  1. Qul huwal laahu ahad
  2. Allah hus-samad
  3. Lam yalid wa lam yoolad
  4. Wa lam yakul-lahu kufuwan ahad

Say, "He is Allah, who is One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born. Nor is there to Him any equivalent."Translation: Saheeh International

الْفَلَق
Al-Falaq
The Daybreak · الْفَلَق

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ﴿١﴾ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ ﴿٢﴾ وَمِنْ شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ ﴿٣﴾ وَمِنْ شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ ﴿٤﴾ وَمِنْ شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ ﴿٥﴾

For grown-ups

  • Print on A4 paper, three bookmarks per page. Thick paper (200 g or more) works best.
  • In the print window, choose "Portrait" and switch "Fit to page" off (scale 100%) so the bookmarks keep their size: 5 × 18 cm.
  • Cut along the dotted lines. Punch the little circle and add a ribbon if you like.
  • The bookmarks carry words of the Quran. Please keep them somewhere clean and respectful.
  • Nothing you type here is sent anywhere. The name is only used to print the bookmarks.
