FAQ

Questions from parents

Everything you may want to know before choosing a book.

What ages are the books for?

The Quran Animals series is written for children aged 5 to 8. Younger children love it too when it is read aloud. The new series Les questions de Malik is for ages 7 to 10.

Which languages are they in?

English, French and Russian.

Are the stories taken from the Quran?

Every book is built around an animal mentioned in the Quran and quotes the exact verse, with the surah name and verse number, so you can check it yourself. Some stories are told as a dream of Yusuf and Maryam. The story of the spider in the cave is an Islamic tradition, and the book presents it as a tradition, not as a verse of the Quran.

What is inside each book?

An illustrated story, the verse from the Quran, fun facts about the animal, questions to talk about together, colouring pages and activities.

Where can I buy the books?

On Amazon, or directly from the author: those copies are signed and posted from France. The Shop page explains both.

How much is shipping?

In France from €5.90 (2–3 working days, straight to your letterbox). In the European Union from €11.50. The calculator on the Shop page shows the exact price for your country and number of books.

Can I get a dedication with my child's name?

Yes. After you order, email us your child's first name and the author will add it to the dedication.

Do you offer prices for schools and Quran or Arabic classes?

Yes, for several copies or full series for a class. Please write to us by email.

Is the kids' corner safe?

Yes. The games are free, with no ads and nothing to buy. We do not collect any data about children: progress stays only on your own device.

When are the next books coming out?

Five new animals, the Malik series and the book Yasmin are on the way. Sign up at the bottom of the home page to hear the day each one comes out.
